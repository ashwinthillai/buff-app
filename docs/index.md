## How buff thinks
1. **Saving is instant.** One box, one Send button. Every note is filed under the day you wrote it.
2. **Organising is automatic and predictable.** Tags, todos and decisions are picked out by simple rules, every time you press Send. No AI is involved in this.
3. **AI is optional and on demand.** Answers, briefs and rewrites only happen when you ask for them.
4. **Your notes are plain text files you own.** They sit in your iCloud Drive and open in any markdown app.
