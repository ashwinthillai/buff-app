**Today** tells you what's waiting. **People & projects** gets you up to speed before you meet someone. Both are worked out from your notes on your device, without AI.

## Today
The first time you open buff each day, it starts on Today (Options → **Start on Today each morning** turns this off). From top to bottom:
- **Search notes:** finds words in every note. With no results, buff suggests what to try next.
- **Write a note:** a quick note without leaving Today, with the same preview, chips and name suggestions as Notes.
- **Your next meeting** (if you've allowed calendar access), see below.
- **Overdue** (in red) and **Due today**: your todos, with their people and projects.
- **Waiting on others:** todos you've given to someone, per person.
- **Recent people & projects:** who and what you've noted lately. Tap one for their page.
- **All todos:** the full list. See [Todos](todos.md#see-your-todos).

## Your next meeting
Tap **Show your meetings here** on Today and allow calendar access. buff then shows your next meeting today or tomorrow with someone you have notes about: their name, what's open between you, and **Brief**, which opens their page. "Later today: 2 more" lists the rest.

- buff matches meetings by attendee email or name, by names in the title (`1:1 Maya`) and by aliases you've taught it.
- When it isn't sure, it asks once: "Is Maya C. → Maya Chen?". **Yes** adds `Maya C.` as an alias in your notes folder (`_index.md`); **no** is remembered on this device.
- **Remind me before meetings** (Options → Calendar) sends a notification 10 minutes before; tap it to open the page.
- buff only reads your calendar. Nothing from it is saved or sent anywhere. If you say no, everything else works and Options → Calendar explains how to turn it on later.

## People & projects
Everyone and every project you've written about, with how many todos are open and when you last noted them. Search at the top. Tap a row for its page.

## Pages and briefs
A page starts with a **plain brief**:
- **Since your last note:** how many days ago you last wrote about them.
- **They owe you** and **You owe them:** open todos either way, matching `/todos`.
- **Open decisions** and the **last notes** about them, newest first.
- **Shared projects** (on a person) or **people** (on a project).

Below the brief come all their notes, by date. Touch and hold a note to **Edit**, **Copy** or **Delete** it (with undo).

With a Claude key, **Write it up** writes a fuller brief from all your notes. See [Living briefs](ai.md#living-briefs-prep). Without a key the button doesn't appear, and the plain brief is always there.

## On iPad
The three screens sit in a sidebar, with **Options** below them. Typed text is kept when you switch screens. With a keyboard, ⌘K opens the command menu.
