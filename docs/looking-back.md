Every note belongs to a day, so looking back is a matter of picking the days.

## One day
- `/today` shows today's notes.
- `/day yesterday`, `/day mon`, `/day -3` (three days ago) or `/day 2026-01-15` shows any other day.

## A week
- `/week` shows this week, day by day.
- `/week -1` is last week, `/week -2` the week before. You can also give a date inside the week you want.
- Choose whether weeks start on Monday or Sunday in Options → Input.

## A month
- `/month` shows one line per day with notes. Tap a day to open it.
- `/month 2026-09` or `/month -1` for other months.
- `/cal` shows a calendar; days with notes are marked with •. Tap a day to open it. `/cal -1` is last month.

## Search
- `/find cafe` searches every note you've written.
- `/find "cost breakdown"` matches the exact phrase.
- Long result lists come a page at a time; `/find more` shows the next page.
- Search also covers documents you've added with `/ingest` (see [Capture from anywhere](capture-anywhere.md#documents)).

## By person or project
To see everything about one person, project or topic instead of one stretch of time, use `/show` (see [People, projects and topics](tags.md#see-everything-about-one)).

## Tips
- Tap any date in the output to open that day.
- Or ask in plain words: `@buff what did I do last week` (see [Ask buff](ask-buff.md)).
