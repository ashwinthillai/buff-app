## Getting started
- buff has three screens: **Today** (what's waiting), **People & projects** (a page for each, with a brief) and **Notes** (where you write).
- Write in plain words and press Send: `send Maya the slides by Friday` becomes a todo due Friday, filed under Maya.
- Type `/tour` for a quick visual tour, or `/help` for the main commands (`/help all` for every one).
- Ask in plain words: `@buff what's waiting for me`.

## Where are my notes?
In the Files app → iCloud Drive → **Buff notes**. They're plain markdown files, so you can open them on a Mac or in any markdown app. [What each file is](docs/vault.md). If iCloud Drive is off, they're kept on your device (Files → On My iPhone/iPad).

## Common questions
- **Can I change or delete a note?** Yes, any note from any day: long-press it → Edit or Delete. Every change can be undone, and the old text is kept in `logs/_history/`. [More](docs/getting-started.md#fix-a-mistake)
- **Where are my todos?** Today → **All todos**, sorted into Overdue, Today, This week, Later, No date and Waiting. Tick to close, swipe to snooze. [More](docs/todos.md)
- **How do I see my meetings?** Tap "Show your meetings here" on Today and allow calendar access. buff only reads; nothing from your calendar is saved. [More](docs/today.md#your-next-meeting)
- **How do I add the widget?** Touch and hold the Home Screen → Edit → Add Widget → buff. [More](docs/todos.md#widgets-and-the-morning-list)
- **How do I add from other apps?** Use Share → buff, say "Add to buff" to Siri, or make a Shortcut with "Add to buff". [More](docs/capture-anywhere.md)
- **Why does an AI feature ask for a key?** AI is on by default where Apple Intelligence is available and runs on your device. Writing up a brief, rewrites and clean-ups use Claude and need your own Anthropic API key (Options → AI → Advanced). Without AI everything else still works. [More](docs/ai.md)
- **Does the developer see my notes?** No. See the [privacy policy](privacy.md).
