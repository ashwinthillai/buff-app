_Last updated: 8 November 2026 (version 2.0)_

buff ("the app") is a notebook for iPhone and iPad. This policy explains what happens to the information you put into it.

## The short version
The developer does not collect, receive, store or sell any of your data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

## Your notes
- Your notes are plain markdown files saved in your own iCloud Drive (the "Buff notes" folder), or on your device if iCloud Drive is off.
- When you edit or delete a note, its earlier text is kept in a history file in the same folder (`logs/_history/`), so you can undo. You can open, keep or delete these files like any other.
- iCloud storage and sync are provided by Apple under Apple's privacy policy. The developer has no access to your iCloud Drive.
- Settings (such as theme and text size) are stored on your device.

## Calendar (optional)
- If you tap "Show your meetings here" and allow access, buff reads today's and tomorrow's events on your device to show your next meeting with someone you have notes about.
- Calendar data is never saved, never written back to your calendar and never leaves your device. The only thing kept is a name you confirm yourself (for example "Maya C. is Maya Chen"), which is added to your own notes folder.
- You can turn calendar access off at any time in Settings → buff.

## Notifications (optional)
Meeting reminders and the morning todo list are local notifications, scheduled on your device. No notification server is involved and nothing is sent anywhere.

## Widgets
The Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets show a short summary of your todos. The summary is kept on your device, in storage shared only between buff and its widget, and is rebuilt from your notes.

## Optional AI features
- **Apple Intelligence (on by default where available):** on supported devices, plain-language requests, quick answers and suggestions after you save a note use Apple's on-device model. This processing happens on your device. Suggestions never change a note until you tap one. You can turn AI off in Options → AI.
- **Claude (your own key):** if you choose to add your own Anthropic API key, some features (such as written-up briefs and cleaning up entries) send the note text needed for that request directly from your device to Anthropic. This is governed by your agreement with Anthropic and Anthropic's privacy policy. The developer never sees the key or the text. Your key is stored in the iOS Keychain on your device. Without a key, nothing is sent. People you mark as restricted are left out of every AI request.

## Sharing into the app
Text, links and files you share into buff from other apps, or save with Siri and Shortcuts, are saved into your notes as described above.

## Children
The app is not directed at children and collects no personal information from anyone.

## Changes
If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
